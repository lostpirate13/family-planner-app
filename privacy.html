Privacy policy

Family Planner is a personal application used only by the family that runs it.

  • It accesses Google Calendar data (calendars and events) of the Google accounts of its own users, only after they grant permission.
  • The data is used only to display and edit that family's own calendar events.
  • Data is held on the family's own private equipment. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train any AI models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
